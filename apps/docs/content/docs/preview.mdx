The [homepage](/#downloads) has a **Preview** button for every asset. It opens the same display used by the desktop edition, rendered on your device with public Hyperliquid market data.

| Edition | Preview |
| --- | --- |
| Bitcoin | [BTC](/preview/?asset=BTC) |
| Solana | [SOL](/preview/?asset=SOL) |
| Ethereum | [ETH](/preview/?asset=ETH) |
| Hyperliquid | [HYPE](/preview/?asset=HYPE) |

No account, wallet, API key, or installation is required. Internet access is needed for fresh prices. The asset's trade QR opens Bloxwap; viewing the screensaver does not place a trade.

## Controls

Move your pointer, tap, or use the keyboard to reveal the controls. Choose **Live**, **5M**, **15M**, or **1H** to change the chart window. Controls fade after four seconds; keyboard focus keeps them visible.

The fullscreen button asks your browser to enter fullscreen after you select it. Use the button again or your browser's fullscreen exit key to leave. When fullscreen is unavailable, the page remains usable in its normal window.

**Keep awake** is opt-in and appears where supported. Your browser or device can refuse the request, and it is released when the page is no longer active. Opening a preview does not turn it on.

Select **Downloads** to return to the website. Direct preview links can be bookmarked or shared.

## Connection and motion

The status pill shows whether the price is live, connecting, delayed, reconnecting, or offline. An interrupted session keeps its last observation and marks it accordingly. A cold offline launch waits for a connection.

The browser may cache the app shell after an online visit. Market prices are never cached or fabricated. Keep your device clock synchronized for reliable freshness checks.

The display respects reduced-motion preferences: the moving grid and continuous chart animation stop while the price and connection status remain available.

## Mobile

On iPhone or iPad, open the preview in Safari and use **Share → Add to Home Screen** when available. On Android, use your browser's install or Add to Home screen action. Home-screen apps launch manually; mobile has no automatic idle activation.

For a local checkout, see [development](/docs/development).
